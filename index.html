Who can participate?+
Anyone — students, professionals, indie hackers, founders. All skill levels welcome.
Is it really free?+
Yes — 100% free. Registration, food, swag, and internet are all provided.
What AI tools are allowed?+
ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf, v0, Bolt.new, Replit AI, Gemini — and similar tools.
Do I need a team?+
No — you can compete solo or bring a team of 2–3. Find teammates at the event too.
What should I bring?+
Your laptop, charger, and your favorite AI tool subscriptions. We provide power, internet, food.
