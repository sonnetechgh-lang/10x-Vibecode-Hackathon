About the Event

What Is
10x Vibecode?

The 10x Vibecode Hackathon is Accra's first AI-only coding competition — a one-day event where developers build real, functional products using nothing but AI tools. No manual coding. No exceptions.

From students writing their first prompt to founders shipping production-ready apps, this is a celebration of AI-first development — the most valuable developer skill of the decade.

Expect intense competition, side games, community energy, and a showcase of what Ghanaian developers can build when they go 10x.

The Golden Rule
Every line of code must be generated by AI. The moment you type code manually, you're out.

FAQ

Common
Questions

Everything you need to know before you register. Still have questions? We're happy to help.

Ask a question →
What does "AI-only" mean exactly?+
Every line of code must be generated by an AI tool — Cursor, ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude, etc. You direct the AI with prompts and review the output, but you cannot manually type or edit code. Judges review your prompt logs.
Do I need to be an experienced developer?+
Not at all. The Beginner track is for students and newcomers. If you can write a clear sentence and describe what you want to build, you can compete. Come and learn.
What can I build?+
Web apps, mobile apps, or AI/ML tools. The challenge theme is revealed on the morning of the event. Your build must relate to the theme. You can combine categories freely.
Can I use paid AI tools?+
Yes. You're responsible for your own subscriptions. We'll share a list of free-tier options that are more than sufficient. Some sponsors may also provide API credits on the day.
What do I need to bring?+
Your laptop, charger, and excitement. We provide the venue, internet, food, and all event materials. Just show up ready to build.
Is there a participation fee?+
Zero. The 10x Vibecode Hackathon is completely free to attend. Registration, food, swag — all included. Access to opportunity shouldn't cost anything.
